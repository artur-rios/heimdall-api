Documentation

Everything needed to understand, run, test, and extend the Heimdall API.

Heimdall is a centralized identity management API. It answers one question for many client systems at once — who is this caller, and what are they allowed to do? — while keeping each client system’s identities isolated from every other’s inside its own scope.

How the documents relate

The requirements documents are the specification — they say what the system must do, and every feature traces back to a numbered requirement (FR-…, NFR-…) and a use case (UC-…). The other sections describe what was built and how to work with it, and cite those identifiers wherever a design decision came from one.
